Cite NEST GPU
=============

Please cite the *version of NEST GPU you used in your work*.

You can find the full citation `on Zenodo <https://zenodo.org/search?q=title%3ANEST%20AND%20GPU%20AND%20-description%3Agraphical%20AND%20simulator&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=publication-desc>`_.
You can also specify the commit hash for work done in the development branches.

Please also cite the relevant NEST GPU publications for which we provide BibTeX entries in our :doc:`publication list <publications>`.

For citing NEST GPU without referring to a specific version, please use :ref:`Golosio et al. (2021) <golosio2021>`.
Even though running under the name NeuronGPU at that time, this is the first article about the library.

You can also `get the NEST GPU logo <https://github.com/nest/nest-gpu/tree/main/doc/htmldoc/static/img/logos>`_ for your poster or presentation.

You can :ref:`let us know <contribute>` about your publications that used NEST GPU, and we
will add them to our :doc:`publication list <publications>`; this will help make them
visible to potential readers.
